# 🛒 E-commerce Marketing & Sales Analytics Case Study

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of an e-commerce business dataset to uncover valuable insights into customer acquisition, retention, marketing effectiveness, revenue generation, and customer behavior. Using Python-based data analysis techniques, the project answers real-world business questions and provides data-driven recommendations for improving business performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to help an e-commerce company make better business decisions by analyzing customer and sales data. The analysis focuses on identifying trends, measuring marketing performance, optimizing customer retention, and increasing overall revenue.

---

## 📊 Business Problems Solved

- Monthly Customer Acquisition Analysis
- Customer Retention Analysis
- Revenue Analysis (New vs Existing Customers)
- Coupon Usage Impact Analysis
- Marketing Spend vs Revenue (ROI)
- Product Performance Analysis
- RFM Customer Segmentation
- Cohort Analysis
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Analysis
- Statistical Hypothesis Testing
- Delivery Charges & Tax Impact Analysis
- Seasonal Sales Analysis
- Daily Sales Trend Analysis
